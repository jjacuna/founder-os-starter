# Project brief: Starter website

This brief is for the made up example company. Edit it to fit your own.

## Goal

A simple one page website that tells people what Sunrise Dog Walking does, what it costs, and how to get in touch.

## Audience

- Busy professionals who work long days
- Older dog owners who need a hand with daily walks

More detail in [customers.md](../../company/customers.md).

## Where the files live

- [website/index.html](../../website/index.html): the page
- [website/styles.css](../../website/styles.css): how it looks

## Sections on the page

1. Top: the name, the tagline, and a button to get in touch
2. About: the mission and a little about Sam, from [overview.md](../../company/overview.md)
3. Services: the three services with prices, from [offerings.md](../../company/offerings.md)
4. Who we help: the two customer groups, from [customers.md](../../company/customers.md)
5. Service area and contact: inner Southeast and Northeast Portland, and the email address

## Rules

- Plain HTML and CSS only. No build step and nothing to install.
- Every fact on the page must come from [company/](../../company/).
- Follow [brand-voice.md](../../company/brand-voice.md).

## Done checklist

- [ ] The page opens when you double click `website/index.html`
- [ ] Names and prices match `company/offerings.md` exactly
- [ ] The copy follows the brand voice (no hype, no exclamation marks)
- [ ] The contact email is correct
- [ ] It reads well on a phone and on a laptop
- [ ] The work is saved in a session note in [history/sessions/](../../history/sessions/)
