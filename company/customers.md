# Customers

Sunrise Dog Walking serves two main groups of dog owners in inner Southeast and Northeast Portland.

## Group 1: Busy professionals

Who they are:

- People who work long days, at an office or at home
- Often away from home, or in meetings, for most of the day

Their problems:

- Their dog is alone for many hours
- They feel guilty and worry about their dog during the day
- They need someone who shows up on time, every time

What they want:

- A walker they can trust with their dog and their home
- Peace of mind that the walk happened

## Group 2: Older dog owners

Who they are:

- Older people who love their dog but need a hand with daily walks

Their problems:

- Long or fast walks are hard on their body
- Bad weather and icy sidewalks make walking risky
- Their dog still needs exercise every day

What they want:

- A steady, friendly helper who comes on a regular schedule
- Simple prices and no surprises

## What both groups care about most

- Reliability: we show up when we say we will
- Trust: the same care every visit
- A happy, tired dog at the end of the day
