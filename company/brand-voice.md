# Brand voice

Brand voice means how the company sounds in writing. Every page, email, and post should sound like the same person.

## Three words

- Warm: we care about the dog and the owner
- Plain: short sentences, everyday words
- Reliable: calm and sure, never pushy

## Words to use

- "your dog"
- "we show up"
- "peace of mind"

## Things to avoid

- Hype (words like "amazing", "best ever", "revolutionary")
- Slang
- "fur baby"
- Exclamation marks

## Examples

Good:

- "We show up on time, and your dog gets a good walk."
- "Peace of mind while you are at work."

Not good:

- "The most AMAZING walks for your fur baby!"
- "We are gonna crush your pup's walkies."

## Quick check before you publish

- Would Sam say this out loud to a neighbor?
- Is every fact also in [overview.md](overview.md) or [offerings.md](offerings.md)?
- Are there any exclamation marks? Remove them.
